# Partner Dashboard

!!! info "Roadmap"
    A self-serve partner dashboard for orders, revenue, refunds, and basic analytics is on the roadmap as a separate product workstream. No commitment date.

## What you can get today

Reach out to `integrations@bluecom.vn` for ad-hoc reports:

- Order list for a date range
- Revenue summary by SKU / country
- Refund and chargeback log

## What the dashboard will cover (planned)

- Order list with filters (date, status, SKU, country)
- Revenue and unit charts
- Refund initiation
- Webhook delivery log and retry
- Theme and locale self-edit

Subscribe to release notes for availability.
